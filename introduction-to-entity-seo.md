## What Is Entity SEO?

Entity SEO is an approach to search optimization that focuses on helping search engines understand identifiable people, organizations, places, products, and other entities.

It involves publishing clear, accurate, and relevant information about an entity and its relationships with other entities.

## Five Core Elements of Entity SEO

### 1. Clear Identity

Use a consistent name, an accurate professional description, and an official website.

### 2. Original Content

Publish useful information that demonstrates your knowledge, professional experience, and expertise.

### 3. Structured Data

Where appropriate, implement valid Schema.org markup that accurately describes information visible on your website.

### 4. Consistent Digital Profiles

Connect your official website with authentic professional profiles, such as LinkedIn and GitHub.

### 5. Independent Recognition

Genuine editorial mentions, relevant citations, and independently documented achievements can provide additional information about an entity.

## Entity SEO and Google Knowledge Panels

Google automatically generates Knowledge Panels using information from various sources.

Structured data, professional profiles, and backlinks can help clarify an entity's identity, but none of them guarantees a Google Knowledge Panel.
